Why Custom WordPress Development?

WordPress powers over 40% of the internet, making it the most popular content management system (CMS) available. However, many developers construct WordPress sites using pre-made themes and stack dozens of plugins on top to achieve basic features. This approach creates security vulnerabilities, slow page speeds, and administrative dashboards that are confusing to manage. Custom theme development solves these issues by coding your site specifically around your content workflows and business needs.

Why is plugin bloat dangerous?

Plugins require continuous updates and often conflict with one another, causing sites to crash. They also load extra style files and scripts across your pages, slowing down mobile load times and hurting search rankings.

WordPress vs Shopify for E-Commerce

For large catalogs or dedicated retail storefronts, we recommend Shopify. For service businesses, content-heavy websites, or hybrid sites with a portfolio and minor merchandise store, we recommend custom WordPress development.
