What Is AI Search Optimization?

AI Search Optimization is the practice of optimizing your digital presence to rank, appear, and get cited in generative AI search experiences (such as ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Google AI Overviews). Unlike traditional search, which ranks links based on keyword density and backlink volume, AI engines compile answers from multiple trusted sources. To be cited, your website must provide highly structured, factual, and easily queryable answer blocks.

How Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Works

We modify your page architectures to match generative processing patterns. This includes implementing citation-native structures (like expert quotes and authority statistics), using explicit entity schemas (defining your founder, industry, and service areas), and deploying llms.txt indexes. These optimizations make it effortless for neural models to extract your data and credit your brand as a trusted source.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the process of optimizing website code and content to increase visibility inside generative AI models. Techniques include adding authoritative citations, statistics, structured tables, and clear summaries to help LLMs easily parse, extract, and recommend your website.

Will GPTBot and PerplexityBot crawl my site?

Yes. If your robots.txt allows access, AI bots will crawl your site to train models and fetch real-time search recommendations. We configure your robots.txt to explicitly welcome verified AI bots while structuring your data to ensure clean, accurate interpretation.
