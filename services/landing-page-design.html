What Is a Custom Landing Page?

Unlike full websites that offer multiple paths and menu options, a landing page is a standalone page designed with a single goal in mind: conversion. Whether you are running paid search campaigns on Google, social media advertisements on Instagram, or launching a specific service product, a tailored landing page strips away distracting options. This structure focuses user attention entirely on filling out your booking form, subscribing to a service, or placing an order.

What makes a landing page convert?

A high-converting landing page relies on relevance, speed, and simplicity. The headline must match the ad copy that drove the click, the page must load in less than two seconds, and the action form must require minimal input fields.

Should I hide my main navigation menu?

Yes. Data shows that keeping navigation menus on landing pages reduces conversion rates. Removing header menus prevents visitors from clicking away to other pages, keeping them focused on the call-to-action.
