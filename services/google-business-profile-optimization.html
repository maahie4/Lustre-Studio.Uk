Why Optimize Your Google Business Profile?

What is Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is a free business listing tool provided by Google. It allows local businesses to manage their presence on Google Maps and standard search pages, displaying reviews, photos, hours, and phone numbers.

How do reviews affect local Map Pack rankings?

Review quantity, rating value, and review velocity are critical ranking indicators. Google prioritizes profiles that consistently receive positive reviews and whose owners actively respond to customer feedback.
