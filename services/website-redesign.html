Why Redesign Your Website?

An outdated website actively turns away customers, positioning your brand as obsolete and forcing you to compete on price rather than quality. Furthermore, search engines penalize older codebases that load slowly or fail to render cleanly on mobile viewports. A website redesign is not just a cosmetic refresh; it is a structural upgrade that aligns your online footprint with modern search requirements and visitor expectations.

How to Redesign Without Losing SEO

Many businesses lose significant traffic after a redesign because their developers change URLs without mapping redirections, causing search crawlers to hit broken pages. We prevent this by crawling your entire domain before the build, mapping every existing URL to its new location, and validating index tags to ensure a smooth transition with zero index drop.

When is it time to redesign your website?

If your website takes more than three seconds to load, is difficult to navigate on smartphones, fails to generate consistent leads, or looks outdated compared to your key competitors, it is time for a full structural redesign.

Will a redesign cause me to lose my Google rankings?

If managed incorrectly, yes. If managed by Lustre Studio using strict URL mapping and index validations, your rankings will remain stable and typically improve post-launch due to faster speeds and cleaner mobile rendering.
