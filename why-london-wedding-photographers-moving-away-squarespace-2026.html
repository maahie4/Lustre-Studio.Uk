Why London Wedding Photographers Are Moving Away From Squarespace in 2026

"A template is a ceiling on your brand's potential."

For years, Squarespace was the default choice for photographers. It was easy, looked "good enough," and handled galleries reasonably well. But in the highly competitive London wedding market of 2026, "good enough" is no longer enough to win high-end bookings.

As photographers move into the premium space—shooting at venues like The Savoy, Hedsor House, or private estates—their digital presence needs to match the sophistication of their work. Here is why the shift toward custom builds is accelerating.

1. The "Template Fatigue" Problem

London couples are discerning. By the time they reach your website, they’ve seen dozens of other photographer portfolios. If your site uses the same "Pacific" or "Brine" template layout as everyone else, you’re subconsciously telling them you’re a commodity, not a specialist.

A custom website removes the "builder look." It allows for unique editorial layouts, sophisticated typography, and a user flow that reflects your specific artistic voice.

2. Performance and Core Web Vitals

Squarespace is a heavy platform. It loads a lot of scripts that you don't need, which slows down your site—especially on mobile. In 2026, speed is a primary ranking factor for Google and a critical factor for user experience.

Custom-coded sites (like the ones we build at Lustre Studio) are lean. They load instantly, keeping potential clients on the page instead of bouncing back to Instagram. When your site is 5x faster than your competitors', you win the SEO battle before it even starts.

3. Conversion Over Content

Most builders are designed to show images, not to convert visitors. A custom site allows us to build "conversion paths"—strategic placements of social proof, inquiry forms, and guided navigation that lead a visitor from "just looking" to "booking a call."
