What is dental SEO?

Dental SEO is the practice of optimizing your clinic's web files to rank for local keywords (like "dentist near me" or "Invisalign London"). It includes local citation building, Google Maps optimization, and writing detailed service pages for each treatment.

Do I need custom photos for my dental website?

Yes. Stock photos of dentists look artificial and lower patient trust. Using real photos of your clinic, team, and smiling patients builds immediate credibility and significantly increases appointment bookings.
