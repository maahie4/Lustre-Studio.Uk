Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 26 August 2026 • Lustre Studio (UK)

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lustre Studio ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate https://lustrestudio.uk (the "Website"), a digital bridal style, beauty, and wedding lifestyle publication. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our readers, subscribers, and community members in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and applicable international privacy frameworks.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information when you interact with our website:

  • Newsletter Subscription Data: When you subscribe to "The Edit" newsletter, we collect your email address and subscription preferences.
  • Contact & Communication Data: When you reach out via our contact form or email, we collect your name, email address, inquiry subject, and the contents of your message.
  • Interactive Tools & Quiz Responses: When you complete our Bridal Style Quiz or budget calculators, answers are processed to produce your recommendations. Aggregated, anonymised results may be used to improve our editorial tools.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: We automatically collect information regarding your browser, IP address, device type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs via cookies and tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate editorial and business purposes:

  • To provide, curate, and maintain our bridal style guides and interactive tools.
  • To send editorial newsletters, curated bridal inspiration, and updates when you have opted in.
  • To respond to your questions, feedback, or partnership submissions.
  • To analyze visitor trends and engagement, allowing us to enhance design quality and content relevance.
  • To ensure website security and prevent fraud or malicious activities.

4. Cookies & Analytics Technologies

We use minimal, performance-oriented tracking tools:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Analyzes aggregated readership metrics, user flow, and content performance.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Helps us understand user interactions (scroll behavior, click heatmaps) to improve our editorial design and layout.

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Services

Lustre Studio participates in curated affiliate marketing programs. If you click on an external link to a bridal designer, retailer, or beauty brand and make a purchase, a tracking cookie may be placed to credit our editorial desk. This does not alter the price you pay nor does it transfer your personal payment information to Lustre Studio. For further details, please review our Affiliate Disclosure.

6. Your Data Rights

Under UK GDPR, you hold specific rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data from our mailing lists and databases.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time via the one-click unsubscribe link at the footer of any email.

To exercise any of these rights or ask questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact our data controller at:
Email: hello@lustrestudio.uk

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect evolving legal standards or editorial features. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
