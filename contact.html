FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with Lustre Studio.

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent projects or tight deadlines, we offer same-day callback availability. Simply note "URGENT" in your message and include your phone number.

What information do I need to provide to get a quote?

At minimum, we need your business name, industry, and a brief description of your goals. To give you the most accurate fixed-price proposal, share your current website (if you have one), the number of pages you need, any specific features (booking, e-commerce, portfolios), your desired timeline, and your approximate budget. The form above captures all of this.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We typically split projects into a 50% deposit to begin, 25% at design approval, and 25% before launch. For projects over 3,000 GBP, we can arrange a 3-payment schedule spread across the project duration. We accept bank transfer, Stripe, and Wise for international clients.

Can we meet before committing?

Absolutely. Every project begins with a free 30-minute discovery call via Zoom or Google Meet. We discuss your goals, audit your current site for technical issues, review competitor positioning, and outline a strategy. No contract is signed until you are completely comfortable with the scope and pricing. As of August 2026, 94% of discovery calls convert into active projects.

What if I need changes after launch?

All projects include a 14-day post-launch support window for bug fixes and minor adjustments. Beyond that, we offer monthly retainer packages starting at 400 GBP/month for ongoing maintenance, content updates, SEO optimization, and conversion rate improvements. Most clients see the best long-term results with at least 3 months of post-launch optimization.

Do you work with clients outside the UK and US?

Yes. While our primary client base is in the UK and US, we have delivered projects for clients in Latvia, Pakistan, Germany, and Australia. All calls are conducted via Zoom, and we accommodate multiple time zones. Invoicing is available in GBP, USD, or EUR.
