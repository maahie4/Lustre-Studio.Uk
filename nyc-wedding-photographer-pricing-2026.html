What's Included in an NYC Wedding Photography Package

When you're comparing quotes from New York City wedding photographers, the listed price is almost always for the base package only. Understanding what's included — and what costs extra — is essential before you sign a contract.

Standard inclusions (almost always included)

  • One lead photographer for the agreed coverage hours
  • Full professional editing and color grading of all selected images
  • Online gallery download (typically 400–800+ images for a full wedding day)
  • High-resolution digital files with personal use rights
  • Travel within the five boroughs

What's typically NOT included

  • A second photographer (add-on, ~$500)
  • Printed albums or physical products
  • Engagement sessions (separate session, ~$450)
  • Aerial/drone footage
  • Travel to The Hamptons, upstate NY, or destination events
  • Rush/expedited gallery delivery
Pro tip for NYC couples

Always ask whether the quote includes travel parking. Manhattan parking can run $30–$80/hour, and some photographers pass this cost to clients. Also confirm the gallery delivery timeline — NYC industry standard is 6–10 weeks, with expedited options available for an additional fee.

When to Book Your NYC Wedding Photographer

New York City operates on an accelerated booking timeline compared to other US markets. The most sought-after photographers — particularly those with editorial credits, Real Weddings publications, and strong social followings — frequently book 18–24 months in advance for peak season dates.

18–24 Months Before
Luxury / editorial photographers — those with Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, or Martha Stewart Weddings features. If your dream photographer is in this tier, inquiry the moment your venue is confirmed.
12–18 Months Before
Peak-season (May–Oct) NYC dates — The most competitive booking window for standard wedding photographers. Cherry blossom season (April) and October foliage dates can book even earlier.
9–12 Months Before
Off-season (Nov–March) dates — Winter NYC weddings have more photographer availability, and some studios offer seasonal pricing advantages for January–March bookings.
6–9 Months Before
Last-chance standard window — Still viable, especially for weekday events or smaller micro-weddings. Elopements can often be booked within 30–90 days with the right team.
Under 3 Months
Rush inquiries — Possible but limiting. Expect narrowed choices, possible rush fees, and reduced negotiating power. Prioritize photographers with explicit availability for your date.

Frequently Asked Questions: NYC Wedding Photography

The questions below are the most-searched wedding photography queries from New York City couples in 2025–2026. We've answered each one in full.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in New York City in 2026?

In 2026, NYC wedding photographer costs range from $1,200 for a 2-hour elopement to $6,500+ for full-day luxury coverage. The average Manhattan couple spends between $3,800 and $5,500 for 8 hours of coverage with a fully edited digital gallery. Brooklyn and Queens run approximately 10–20% lower. Destination weddings in the Hamptons or upstate can exceed $12,000 once travel and multi-day premiums are applied.

What is included in a typical NYC wedding photography package?

A standard NYC wedding photography package includes a set number of coverage hours (6–10 is most common), one lead photographer, full professional editing of all selected images, a private online gallery for downloading high-resolution files, and personal use rights to your images. Add-ons like a second photographer (+$500), engagement session (+$450), aerial drone coverage (+$350), heirloom album (+$800), and expedited 14-day gallery delivery (+$300) are typically available separately.

How far in advance should I book a wedding photographer in NYC?

Book your NYC wedding photographer at least 12–18 months in advance for peak season (May–October) dates. Luxury and editorial-tier photographers fill their calendars 18–24 months out. For off-season winter weddings, 6–9 months may be sufficient. Central Park elopements are sometimes available within 30–60 days, depending on permit availability and photographer schedules.

Do NYC wedding photographers charge extra for travel?

Most NYC-based photographers include travel within the five boroughs in their base rate. Travel to The Hamptons, upstate New York, New Jersey venues, or international destinations typically adds 15–25% to the package total, plus accommodation costs for overnight events. Always confirm travel policy and any parking reimbursement requirements before signing a contract.

Can I get drone photography at my NYC wedding?

Yes, but with important caveats. Drone photography within New York City requires FAA Part 107 certification from your photographer and specific NYC Parks permits or airspace authorization via LAANC. Most of Manhattan (below 400ft) sits in controlled airspace. Drone coverage is most commonly done at venues in Long Island, the Hudson Valley, New Jersey, or the Hamptons where airspace restrictions are less severe. Always confirm regulatory compliance before booking this add-on.

How long does it take to receive photos from a NYC wedding photographer?

The NYC industry standard for gallery delivery is 6–10 weeks after your wedding date. Photographers in peak season (June–October) may take up to 12 weeks. Expedited delivery (within 14 days) is available through most studios for an additional $300 fee. A preview set of 10–20 images is often shared within 48–72 hours as a courtesy.
