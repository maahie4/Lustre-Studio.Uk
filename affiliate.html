Our Commitment to Editorial Integrity • Lustre Studio (UK)

1. Editorial Independence

At Lustre Studio (https://lustrestudio.uk), our mission is to deliver quiet luxury, discerning aesthetic direction, and elevated bridal style curation. Our editorial recommendations for wedding gowns, bridal veils, jewelry, footwear, and beauty are guided solely by design excellence, craftsmanship, and real aesthetic value to our readers.

2. How Affiliate Links Work

Some of the links featured across our guides, product roundups, and curation edits are "affiliate links." This means that if you click on a recommended link and subsequently complete a purchase on a merchant's website, Lustre Studio may receive a small commission from the retailer or affiliate network.

  • No Cost to You: Clicking or purchasing through an affiliate link never increases the price you pay. Pricing, promotions, and discounts remain identical.
  • Unbiased Selection: We never select or feature bridal items solely to earn a commission. Items are chosen by our editorial team because they align with our modern luxury ethos.
  • Support for Free Content: Affiliate commissions allow us to keep all Lustre Studio guides, interactive style quizzes, and planning tools completely free and accessible without intrusive advertising.

3. Compliance with Advertising Standards

In strict compliance with the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA), the Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP) code, and the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines, we clearly disclose commercial relationships. Any sponsored editorial content or dedicated brand collaborations are always clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "In Partnership With" at the top of the feature.

4. Brand & Vendor Partnerships

We welcome relationships with emerging bridal designers, couture ateliers, and luxury beauty houses whose aesthetics resonate with Lustre Studio. However, we retain full editorial control over all published critiques, pairings, and selections.
