Photo Booth Booking Portal

Event Services · Manchester, UK · Completed June 2026

Outcome
340% more online bookings 1.4s load time premium positioning

The Challenge

The client was relying on a generic Wix template with a clunky third-party booking widget that redirected users off-site to complete reservations. Mobile users accounted for 78% of traffic, yet the bounce rate sat at 71% due to poor thumb-friendly navigation and a 4.2-second load time. There was no payment integration, meaning every inquiry required manual follow-up via WhatsApp — leading to lost leads outside business hours.

Our Approach

We designed a completely custom booking architecture with real-time availability logic, Stripe payment integration, and an automated confirmation flow via SMS and email. The mobile experience was rebuilt from the ground up with thumb-friendly navigation, sticky call-to-action bars, and instant-load service pages. We also implemented local SEO markup targeting "photo booth hire Manchester" and surrounding areas.

Key Features

  • Real-time booking calendar with live availability
  • Integrated Stripe payment processing with deposit option
  • Automated SMS and email confirmation workflows
  • Mobile-optimized service routing and sticky CTAs
  • Google Business Profile sync for local search visibility

The Results

Within 60 days of launching in June 2026, completed online bookings increased by 340%. Mobile bounce rate dropped from 71% to 23%. The site now loads in 1.4 seconds and ranks on page 1 for "photo booth hire Manchester" and three related long-tail keywords. The client no longer loses after-hours inquiries — 42% of bookings now come through outside standard business hours.

Handcrafted Furniture Shop

E-Commerce · London, UK · Completed May 2026

Outcome
156% revenue increase 1.1s load time 210% more commissions

The Challenge

An established furniture maker was constrained by a rigid Shopify theme that could not accommodate their bespoke commission workflow. The site loaded in 3.8 seconds, cart abandonment sat at 68%, and the mobile checkout process required 7 steps. Product imagery was compressed poorly by the theme, failing to communicate the tactile quality of handcrafted wood and upholstery. The brand was losing high-value commission inquiries to competitors with cleaner digital experiences.

Our Approach

We developed a custom Shopify storefront with a bespoke product configurator for commission pieces, allowing customers to select wood types, dimensions, upholstery, and finishes with real-time pricing updates. The checkout was streamlined to 3 steps on mobile. We implemented aggressive performance optimization including critical CSS inlining, next-gen image formats (AVIF/WebP), and deferred JavaScript loading. A dedicated wholesale portal was added for interior designers and trade clients.

Key Features

  • Bespoke product configurator with real-time pricing for commission pieces
  • 3-step mobile checkout with Apple Pay and Google Pay integration
  • AR preview integration for viewing furniture in room context
  • Password-protected wholesale portal with trade pricing tiers
  • Performance-optimized asset delivery with AVIF and responsive images

The Results

Revenue increased by 156% in the first 60 days post-launch. Cart abandonment reduced from 68% to 31%. Load time decreased to 1.1 seconds, earning a perfect Core Web Vitals score. Custom commission inquiries — the highest-margin segment of the business — increased by 210%. The wholesale portal now accounts for 28% of total monthly revenue, opening an entirely new revenue stream that did not exist on the previous site.

Method

How Every Project Works

We do not use templates. Every site is designed, coded, and optimized from a blank canvas. Here is the exact process that produces the results you see above.

Step 01

Discovery & Audit

We conduct a deep-dive into your brand, competitors, and existing analytics. We define success metrics — whether that is booking conversions, call volume, or e-commerce revenue — and audit your current site for technical SEO gaps, speed bottlenecks, and UX friction points. This phase typically takes 2-3 days.

Step 02

Architecture & Wireframes

We map the complete user journey and build the semantic HTML structure that search engines will love. Wireframes are created in-browser, not in Figma — so you see your actual site taking shape from day one. Information architecture, URL structure, and conversion funnels are locked before a single visual element is designed.

Step 03

Design & Build

Visual design happens directly in the browser. No mockup surprises — you see real typography, real imagery, and real interactions on actual devices. Every page is built for speed, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA), and conversion. Custom functionality — booking flows, configurators, portals — is developed and tested during this phase. Typically 2-4 weeks.

Step 04

Launch & Optimize

We run a comprehensive performance audit, optimize Core Web Vitals to green across all metrics, configure search engine indexing, and implement conversion tracking via Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Post-launch, we monitor for 14 days and make micro-adjustments to maximize early performance. Most sites go live in 4-6 weeks total.
