Method

How We Deliver Every Service

Whether you need a single landing page or a full e-commerce ecosystem, our process ensures nothing is missed and every decision is backed by strategy.

Step 01

Discovery & Strategy

We audit your current digital presence, analyze competitors, and define measurable success metrics. This phase determines which services you actually need and which can wait.

Step 02

Architecture & Scope

We map the complete sitemap, URL structure, and conversion funnel. Wireframes are built in-browser so you see real structure before visual design begins. Scope and pricing are locked here.

Step 03

Design & Development

Visual design happens directly in code. You review on real devices, not static mockups. Custom functionality — booking flows, SEO architecture, e-commerce logic — is built and tested during this phase.

Step 04

Launch & Optimize

We run final performance audits, configure search indexing, set up analytics tracking, and monitor for 14 days post-launch. Most projects go live in 4-6 weeks. Retainer clients receive continuous optimization thereafter.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, process, and how we work with clients across the US and UK.

Do you use templates or page builders?

Never. Every site is built from a blank canvas with hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This gives us complete control over performance, SEO structure, and visual design. We do not use WordPress page builders, Shopify themes, or drag-and-drop tools. The result is a faster, more secure, and more unique website that no competitor can replicate.

Which platforms do you build on?

We build custom static sites, WordPress themes, and Shopify storefronts. For e-commerce, we specialize in Shopify with custom theme development in Liquid. For content-heavy sites, we use headless WordPress or fully custom CMS solutions. For brochure sites and portfolios, we often recommend static builds for maximum speed and security. The right platform is determined during discovery based on your content needs, budget, and growth plans.

How is your SEO different from other agencies?

SEO is baked into the foundation, not bolted on later. We build semantic HTML, optimize Core Web Vitals from day one, and structure content for both traditional search and AI engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Most agencies treat SEO as a separate monthly service that starts after launch. We treat it as a design constraint — every page, every image, every line of code is optimized before the site goes live. As of August 2026, 89% of our SEO clients rank on page 1 for their primary target keywords within 90 days.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Absolutely. While many clients bundle design and SEO for maximum impact, every service is available standalone. We will recommend the right combination based on your goals during the discovery call. If you already have a great site but need SEO, we will audit it and optimize. If you need a new site but already have an SEO strategy, we will build to your specifications. No minimum commitments and no forced bundles.

What industries do you specialize in?

We have deep experience with photographers, dental and medical practices, home services (plumbing, HVAC, roofing), legal professionals, coaches and consultants, e-commerce brands, cosmetic and aesthetic clinics, and automotive detailing. That said, our process adapts to any industry that values premium design and measurable search visibility. The principles of fast load times, clear conversion paths, and semantic SEO apply universally.

How long does a typical project take?

Most custom sites launch within 4-6 weeks from kickoff. Complex e-commerce builds with custom configurators or multi-location service sites may extend to 8 weeks. SEO campaigns begin showing measurable results within 60-90 days. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase and stick to it. Rush projects are available for an additional fee.
